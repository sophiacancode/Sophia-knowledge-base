# Kitten Health and Wellness

This section focuses on maintaining a kitten's physical health and wellbeing. This provides information on preventative care, recognizing signs of illness, maintaining hygiene, and creating a safe and healthy environment for kittens.

## Topics

- Veterinary Care: Vaccinations, routine checkups, deworming, and spaying or neutering.

- Common Health Issues: Recognizing symptoms of diarrhea, vomiting, respiratory infections, and parasites.

- Grooming and Hygiene: Bathing, nail trimming, dental care, ear cleaning, and litter box maintenance.

- Physical Activity and Exercise: Playtime, toys, exercise, and healthy activity levels.

- Mental and Emotional Wellness: Socialization, reducing stress, affection, and environmental enrichment.

- Preventative Care: Flea and tick prevention, monitoring weight, and recognizing changes in behavior.

Purpose: To help kitten owners understand their kitten's health needs, recognize potential health concerns early, and establish routines.
