# Kitten Safety and Environment

This section focuses on creating a safe, comfortable, and healthy living environment for kittens. It provides information on household accidents, identifying potential hazards, and providing an environment that supports healthy development.
## Topics
- Toxic Plants and Household Hazards: Identifying poisonous plants, cleaning products, chemicals, and other dangerous substances.

- Safe Sleeping Spaces: Creating comfortable resting areas, providing appropriate bedding, and establishing a safe space.

- Safe Toys and Accessories: Choosing toys and identifying choking or injury hazards

- Emergency Preparedness: Eliminating and knowing when to contact a veterinarian durning an emergency. 

Purpose: To help kitten owners create a secure and comfortable home, prevent common accidents, and protect their kittens from environmental dangers.
