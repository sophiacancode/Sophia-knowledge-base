# Litter Box and Hygiene

This section focuses on maintaining a clean litter box and good animal hygiene to support a kitten's health and comfort. It provides guidance on litter box training, choosing appropriate/ best rated litter, maintaining a clean litter box area, and regular hygiene routines.

## Topics

- Litter Box Training: Teaching kittens to use the litter box based on age and establishing consistent habits.

- Choosing Litter: Comparing litter types, textures, and safe options. 

- Litter Box Maintenance: Scooping, cleaning, replacing litter, and preventing unpleasant odors

- Grooming Hygiene: Brushing fur, trimming nails, cleaning ears, and dental care.

- Accidents and Odor Control: Addressing litter box accidents.

- Sanitation and Cleanliness: Cleaning food bowls, bedding, toys, and frequently used areas/ food and water bowls.

Purpose: To help kitten owners maintain hygiene routines.
